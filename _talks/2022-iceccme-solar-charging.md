Presentation of a portable solar charging system combining Perturb and Observe (P&O) Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) with Constant Current–Constant Voltage (CC-CV) battery charging.

Overview
======

The research investigates the integration of MPPT and battery charging control algorithms to improve the efficiency of portable solar-powered charging systems.

Key Topics
======

* Photovoltaic energy conversion
* Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT)
* Perturb and Observe (P&O) algorithm
* Constant Current–Constant Voltage (CC-CV) charging
* Energy-efficient power management

Research Contribution
======

Design and evaluation of a combined control approach for efficient solar energy harvesting and battery charging.
