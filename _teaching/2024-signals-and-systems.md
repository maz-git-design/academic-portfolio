Practical laboratory instruction in signal processing and linear systems.

Course Overview
======

Introduction to the analysis and simulation of continuous-time and discrete-time signals and systems.

Topics Covered
======

* Signal representation and classification
* Linear time-invariant (LTI) systems
* Convolution and system response
* Fourier and Laplace transforms
* Sampling and discrete-time signals

Teaching Activities
======

* MATLAB and Python simulations
* Laboratory exercises and demonstrations
* Practical assignments and student supervision
