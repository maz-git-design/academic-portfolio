Laboratory instruction in control systems analysis and simulation.

Course Overview
======

Fundamental concepts of feedback control, dynamic system modelling, and stability analysis.

Topics Covered
======

* Mathematical modelling of dynamic systems
* Transfer functions and block diagrams
* Open-loop and closed-loop systems
* System stability and transient response
* PID control and controller design

Teaching Activities
======

* MATLAB-based system modelling and simulation
* Practical laboratory sessions
* Engineering exercises and student project support
