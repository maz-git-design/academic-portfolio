Teaching assistance and laboratory instruction in electricity, electric circuits, and electromagnetism.

Course Overview
======
Fundamental principles of electricity, electric circuits, and electromagnetic phenomena.

Topics Covered
======
* Electrostatics and electric fields
* Electric potential and capacitance
* DC and AC electrical circuits
* Magnetic fields and electromagnetic induction
* Fundamental electromagnetic laws

Teaching Activities
======
* Tutorials and problem-solving sessions
* Laboratory experiments
* Student assessment and technical support
